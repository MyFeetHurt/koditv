KODItv – Privacy policy

KODItv is used only to let a personal Kodi media center access the signed-in user's own YouTube account (subscriptions, playlists, watch history) through the YouTube Data API.

  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the user's own Kodi device and can be removed by signing out in the add-on or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
